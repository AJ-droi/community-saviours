Company Profile

Transforming Lives Through Music, Creativity and Opportunity



1. WHO WE ARE
Community Saviours CIC is a community-focused organisation established to use
music and creative arts as tools for rehabilitation, prevention, personal
development, and social reintegration.

We believe that creativity has the power to unlock potential, restore confidence,
develop discipline, and create meaningful pathways towards education, employment,
entrepreneurship, and positive participation in society.

Our programmes are designed primarily to support individuals affected by the criminal
justice system, including people in custodial settings and those preparing to reintegrate
into society. We also work preventatively with at-risk young people, marginalised
communities, individuals experiencing social exclusion, and people participating
in rehabilitation and recovery programmes.

Through structured music and creative development programmes, we provide
participants with opportunities to discover their abilities, develop practical skills, build
confidence, and explore positive futures.

Community Saviours CIC operates for community benefit and not for private profit.
Any surplus generated through our activities will be reinvested into the organisation to
support and expand our social impact.
2. OUR VISION
Our vision is:

To create safer, stronger and more inclusive communities by transforming lives
through music, creativity, skills development, rehabilitation and opportunity.

We envision a society where individuals affected by imprisonment, disadvantage,
exclusion, or challenging life circumstances are given meaningful opportunities to
rebuild their confidence, develop their potential, and contribute positively to their
communities.




3. OUR MISSION
Our mission is to use music, creativity, mentoring and vocational development to
support rehabilitation, prevent social exclusion, and create pathways towards positive
reintegration.

We aim to:

   •   Deliver meaningful music and creative programmes within prisons and custodial
       settings.
   •   Support individuals transitioning from custody back into the community.
   •   Provide creative, emotional and personal development opportunities.
   •   Build confidence, discipline, communication and teamwork skills.
   •   Develop practical and vocational skills connected to the creative and music
       industries.
   •   Support at-risk young people through preventative and early-intervention
       programmes.
   •   Reduce social exclusion and encourage positive community participation.
   •   Create progression pathways into education, employment, entrepreneurship
       and creative careers.
   •   Build partnerships with prisons, rehabilitation organisations, schools,
       community groups and other stakeholders.
4. OUR PURPOSE
Community Saviours CIC exists to demonstrate that creativity can be a powerful
vehicle for personal transformation and social change.

Many individuals affected by the criminal justice system or social exclusion face
significant barriers when attempting to rebuild their lives. These barriers may include
low confidence, limited qualifications, unemployment, isolation, lack of positive
networks, and limited access to meaningful opportunities.

Our programmes aim to address these challenges by providing a constructive
environment where participants can:

   •   Express themselves creatively.
   •   Discover and develop their talents.
   •   Build confidence and self-belief.
   •   Learn practical and transferable skills.
   •   Develop positive relationships and teamwork abilities.
   •   Explore future educational and employment opportunities.
   •   Build a stronger sense of identity and purpose.

We believe that when people are given the right tools, encouragement and
opportunities, they can develop the confidence and skills required to create positive
change in their own lives and communities.
